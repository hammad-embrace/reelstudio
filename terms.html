1. What Reel Studio is

Reel Studio, by Art by HM, is a desktop application that edits short vertical videos and, when its user asks it to, publishes or schedules them to the user's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API, and to the user's own Facebook Page through Meta's Graph API.

2. Your accounts

You connect each account yourself on the platform's own login page. Reel Studio uploads and publishes videos only to the accounts you connected, with the caption, privacy setting and time you chose. Nothing is posted without an explicit action from you: "Post now" or a schedule you set. You can disconnect at any time in Reel Studio's settings, in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, or in Facebook under Settings → Business integrations.

3. Your content

You are responsible for everything you publish and must follow TikTok's Terms of Service, its Community Guidelines and its Music Usage Confirmation, as well as Meta's terms, including having the rights to any third-party material you use.
