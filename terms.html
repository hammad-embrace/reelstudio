1. What Reel Studio is

Reel Studio is a personal tool that runs on its owner's computer. It edits short vertical videos and, when the owner asks it to, publishes or schedules them to the owner's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

2. Who may use it

Reel Studio is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public, and it never posts to any TikTok account other than the one its owner connected.

3. Your TikTok account

Connecting a TikTok account lets Reel Studio upload videos and publish them with the caption and privacy setting chosen at the time. Nothing is posted without an explicit action, either "Post now" or a schedule set by the owner. You can disconnect at any time, either in Reel Studio's settings or under Security > Manage app permissions in the TikTok app.

4. Content

The account owner is responsible for everything they publish, and must follow TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, including the rights to any third-party material they use.
