Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

What data Reel Studio uses

When you connect a TikTok account, Reel Studio receives from TikTok:

  • an access token and a refresh token, used to upload and publish videos;
  • basic profile information (display name, username, avatar), shown so you can see which account will post;
  • the posting options TikTok allows for that account (privacy choices, maximum video length), and the status of your uploads.

When you connect a Facebook Page, Reel Studio receives the Page's name, picture and a Page access token, used to publish reels to that Page.

Where it is kept

Everything stays on the computer where Reel Studio runs. Tokens are kept in files only that computer's user can read. Reel Studio has no server and no database, and it does not collect analytics.

Sharing

Reel Studio does not sell or share any data. It sends videos and captions only to the platform you publish to, and only when you publish.

Other users

Reel Studio does not collect any information about other TikTok or Facebook users or viewers.

Deleting your data

"Disconnect" in Reel Studio's settings revokes the token with the platform and deletes it from the computer. You can also remove access in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, or in Facebook under Settings → Business integrations.
