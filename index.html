Turn long videos into scroll-stopping reels

Reel Studio is the desktop studio behind Art by HM. It finds the strongest moments in a long video, builds vertical 9:16 reels with a hook headline, captions and an optional AI voiceover, and publishes or schedules them to TikTok and Facebook.

How it works

From a long video to a published reel in a few minutes.

Load a videoPaste a link to a long video; Reel Studio downloads it with its subtitles.
Pick and edit the momentsAccept the suggested moments or choose your own, then edit the hook, clip times and narration, with a live preview.
Build the reelsRender upload-ready 9:16 videos with captions and voiceover.
Connect your accountsLog in with TikTok and Facebook through their official login screens. You choose exactly what Reel Studio may do.
Publish or scheduleReview the caption, choose who can see the post and when it goes out, and confirm. Reel Studio shows the result when the platform finishes processing.

How Reel Studio uses TikTok

Built on TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

Login Kit

You connect your account on TikTok's own login page. Reel Studio shows your display name and avatar so you always know which account will post.

Direct Post

Before every post you see the caption, choose who can see it (there is no default), set comments, Duet and Stitch, and confirm. Nothing is posted without your click.

Upload to inbox

Or send the reel to your TikTok inbox as a draft, then edit and post it yourself in the TikTok app.

FAQ

Who can use Reel Studio?

Reel Studio is made and used by Art by HM. It runs on the creator's own computer, and every account it posts to is connected by its owner through the platform's official login.

Does Reel Studio post without asking?

No. A post goes out only when you click “Post now” or set a schedule yourself, and scheduled posts can be cancelled at any time before they go out.

What data does it store?

Only the login tokens the platforms issue and the posting status, stored on the creator's computer. Nothing is sold or shared. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I disconnect my account?

Use “Disconnect” in Reel Studio's settings, or remove the app in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
